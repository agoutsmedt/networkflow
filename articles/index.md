# Articles

### Guides

- [From references to
  networks](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/articles/from-references-to-networks.md):

  What a projected network is, how a table of citations becomes a
  bibliographic coupling network, which similarity measure and threshold
  to choose, and how to cut a corpus into time windows.

- [Finding and reading
  clusters](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/articles/finding-and-reading-clusters.md):

  What community detection does, how the Leiden algorithm and its
  resolution shape the clusters, how to check their stability, and how
  to name, characterise and draw them.

- [Following clusters over
  time](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/articles/following-clusters-over-time.md):

  How to detect clusters in each time window, link them across windows
  into dynamic clusters, and draw their history as an alluvial plot.

- [Beyond bibliographic
  coupling](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/articles/beyond-bibliographic-coupling.md):

  The same pipeline for co-citation, for authors instead of articles,
  and for any two-mode table; and why the identifiers of references
  matter.
