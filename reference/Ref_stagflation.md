# Articles and Books Explaining the 1970s US Stagflation.

A dataset containing all the articles and books cited by the books and
academic articles endeavouring to explain what happened in the US
economy in the 1970s.

## Usage

``` r
Ref_stagflation
```

## Format

A data frame with 4416 rows and 6 variables:

- source_id:

  Identifier of the citing document

- target_id:

  Identifier of the cited document

- target_author:

  Author of the cited document

- target_year:

  Year of publication of the cited document

- target_title:

  Title of the cited document

- target_journal:

  Journal of publication of the cited document (if an article)

## Source

Goutsmedt A. (2020) “From Stagflation to the Great Inflation: Explaining
the 1970s US Economic Situation”. Revue d’Economie Politique,
Forthcoming 2021.
