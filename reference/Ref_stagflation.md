# References cited by the documents explaining the 1970s US stagflation

The direct citations of the corpus of Goutsmedt (2021) : one row per
link between a document of
[Nodes_stagflation](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/reference/Nodes_stagflation.md)
explaining the stagflation and a document it cites. It is the bipartite
relation that
[`build_network()`](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/reference/build_network.md)
and
[`build_dynamic_networks()`](https://agoutsmedt.github.io/networkflow/reference/build_dynamic_networks.md)
project into a bibliographic coupling network.

## Usage

``` r
Ref_stagflation
```

## Format

A data frame with 4,416 rows and 6 variables:

- source_id:

  Identifier of the citing document.

- target_id:

  Identifier of the cited document.

- target_author:

  Author of the cited document.

- target_year:

  Year of publication of the cited document.

- target_title:

  Title of the cited document.

- target_journal:

  Journal of the cited document, for articles.
